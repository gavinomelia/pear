Pear

Screen sharing that isn't trash.

Share your Mac at native resolution, up to 60 fps, with a pointer that keeps up and a pen for everyone in the call.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

First launch

To share your screen, turn on Pear in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then reopen it. macOS only applies that permission after a relaunch.
